The UFO specification has been built and refined over many years through the combined effort of type designers, software developers, and the broader type community. From its original authors to the many people who have submitted issues, pull requests, and discussion threads, this project is the product of genuine collaborative work.

## Community Contributors

The UFO specification is a community project with input and edits from many people. The full list of GitHub contributors — with their commit counts and profiles — is available on the [GitHub contributors page](https://github.com/unified-font-object/ufo-spec/graphs/contributors).

<Note>
Community contributions include code, documentation edits, issue reports, and discussion. If you have participated in the specification process in any form, your input is valued and appreciated.
</Note>
